Features at a Glance

Six integrated systems working simultaneously — all controlled from one app on any smartphone.

📷
CAM
Live Camera
ESP32-CAM

Streams real-time video to the operator's smartphone for remote visual assessment of every corner.

⚠️
GAS
Gas Detection
MQ2 Sensor

Detects smoke and toxic gases, triggers instant alerts before any worker entry into the zone.

🌡️
ENV
Temp & Fire Monitor
DHT11 + Flame Sensor

Provides real-time temperature and fire detection readings. Alerts on critical thresholds.

📍
GPS
GPS Tracking
NEO-6M Module

Sends live coordinates to the dashboard. Always know ARES's exact position in the field.

📡
NAV
Obstacle Detection
Ultrasonic Sensor

Prevents collision in dark, narrow spaces. Navigates rubble without manual intervention.

💊
MED
Medicine Delivery
Servo-Controlled Box

Delivers first aid to trapped survivors with one button press. No human contact needed.

All controlled from one app and server — Blynk IoT and open server — on any Android or iOS smartphone.
